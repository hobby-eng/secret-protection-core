# Third-party notices

Runtime dependencies: zeroize 1.9.1 (MIT OR Apache-2.0, by the RustCrypto developers), which wipes secret bytes with writes the compiler keeps; on Unix systems libc 0.2.191 (MIT OR Apache-2.0, by the Rust project developers), the declarations of the system calls; on Windows windows-sys 0.61.2 (MIT OR Apache-2.0, by Microsoft), the declarations of the Windows API, and windows-link 0.2.1 (MIT OR Apache-2.0, by Microsoft), which links them.

On Linux, landlock 0.4.7 (MIT OR Apache-2.0, copyright Mickaël Salaün, the author of Landlock, developed by the Landlock project) builds the Landlock layer of `Isolation::enter`. It replaces hand-written system calls and kernel structures: the project that maintains Landlock in the kernel also knows the rights of each new ABI, and its best-effort mode leaves out on an older kernel what that kernel does not know. Linux-only, without a C library. It brings enumflags2 0.7.12 (MIT OR Apache-2.0, by Maik Klein and others) and thiserror 2.0.21 (MIT OR Apache-2.0, by David Tolnay), and, at build time only, the macros of those two with proc-macro2 1.0.107, quote 1.0.47, syn 2.0.119 and 3.0.10 (each MIT OR Apache-2.0, by David Tolnay) and unicode-ident 1.0.27 ((MIT OR Apache-2.0) AND Unicode-3.0, by David Tolnay).
